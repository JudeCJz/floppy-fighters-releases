HOW TO USE
Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT (image generation). Save the result as:
  website/img/website_art.png
The site picks it up automatically: each quarter of the sheet fills one spot on the page
(top-left = hero, top-right = online mode, bottom-left = crate rain / storm, bottom-right = armory).
If the quarters come out with borders or gaps, crop each one and save them instead as
  website/img/hero.png, website/img/online.png, website/img/storm.png, website/img/armory.png
(any of these overrides its quarter of the sheet).

----- PROMPT -----

Create ONE landscape image, 1536x1024, split into a 2x2 grid of four separate illustrations. Each illustration fills exactly one quarter (768x512 each), edge to edge, with NO borders, NO gutters, NO frames and NO text, letters or logos anywhere.

Style for all four: bold 2D cartoon game art for a mobile ragdoll brawler called Floppy Fighters. Thick clean dark outlines, flat cel shading, punchy contrast. Palette: deep red (#d8262c), near-black ink (#1b1b1f), off-white paper (#f4f3f1), with warm orange/yellow only for fire and explosions and a little cold blue for lightning. Characters are simple stick-figure fighters: a big round head with no face (or one tiny black dot eye), thin rounded capsule limbs and torso, floppy ragdoll poses, each fighter a single solid colour (red, black, white, and one blue). Original designs only. Do not imitate any existing game, character or brand.

1. TOP-LEFT - "Hero": a red stick fighter and a black stick fighter mid-brawl on a crumbling cliff ledge over a red-misted canyon with distant castle towers. The red fighter swings a sword, the black one is flying backwards, limbs flopping, from a shotgun blast. A rocket trail and sparks cross the sky. Keep the LEFT third calmer and darker (mostly sky and haze) because page text sits there; the action is on the right two thirds.

2. TOP-RIGHT - "Online mode, coming soon": four stick fighters (red, black, white, blue) each standing on a separate floating island platform, linked to each other by glowing red signal arcs over a dark night sky with a huge faint globe made of red grid lines behind them. Feels like a teaser: mysterious, glowing, exciting. Leave the RIGHT side of the image darker and emptier (text sits there on desktop).

3. BOTTOM-LEFT - "Crate rain and thunderstorm": a dark stormy wasteland arena at night, heavy diagonal rain, one bright lightning bolt splitting the sky, wooden crates, steel crates and red TNT crates falling from the clouds with motion streaks, two tiny stick fighters below running and diving for cover, one TNT crate exploding on impact. Keep the CENTRE band a bit darker for a headline over it.

4. BOTTOM-RIGHT - "The armory": a dark industrial weapon wall seen straight on, softly lit with red rim light: cartoon pistol, revolver, shotgun, sniper, minigun, rocket launcher, sword, spear, bat, a pile of grenades, a glowing purple black-hole grenade, a blue freeze ray and a coconut launcher, all hanging on hooks in neat rows. Low contrast and slightly blurred toward the edges so it works as a background behind cards.
