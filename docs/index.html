WHAT'S IN IT

LAN multiplayer

One phone hosts, everyone on the same Wi-Fi joins in a tap. No accounts, no internet. If the host leaves, the match carries on.

28 weapons

Pistols, snipers, swords, spears, rockets, a nuke, a coconut launcher and grenades that freeze, burn, gas or suck everyone into a black hole.

Maps & themes

50+ hand-built maps across six themes, plus random Wild Lands maps that are different every round. Seesaws, spinners, lifts and ice.

Crate rain

Some rounds the sky drops crates. Stand on them, hide behind them, or shoot the TNT ones next to your friends.

Thunderstorms

Rain and lightning roll in on random rounds. The rain puts fire out faster, so plan your fire grenades.

Power-ups

Crack a lucky block for homing bullets, invisibility, a Totem of Life or a rare weapon. Gift boxes pop open into something random.
