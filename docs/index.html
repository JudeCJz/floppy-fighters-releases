The game

Everything in the box

Online play

Host a game and anyone on the internet can join it from the list. Add a password to keep it to your friends.

Wi-Fi games

Phones on the same Wi-Fi find each other on their own. Up to 16 players in one match.

Voice chat

Hold TALK, or switch to open mic. A speaker shows next to whoever is talking. Light on data.

Quick chat

A wheel of ready lines: hold, slide, let go. Write your own lines in settings.

28 weapons

Guns, blades, grenades, launchers, a flamethrower and a laser. Throw any of them when you're done.

Mystery Blocks

Hit one and find out what falls out: a power-up, a weapon or a surprise.

52 arenas

Six worlds of hand-made maps with lifts, swings and crates, plus a new generated map every round.

CPUs and teams

Fill the match with CPUs at four levels. Free-for-all or red against blue.

Sandbox

Spawn any weapon, place training dummies and switch map events on and off.

Your controls

Move, resize and set the opacity of every button. On a computer, set your own keys.

    Help

    Installing

    How do I install the APK?

    Download it on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.

    Play Protect warns about the app

    Stickshot isn't on the Play Store, so Play Protect doesn't know it yet. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

    How do I update?

    The app shows an UPDATE button on the main menu when a new version is out. The browser version updates itself.

    My friend can't see my online game

    Both of you need the same version. Update, then look in Available games, or tap Refresh.

    Voice chat doesn't hear me

    Allow the microphone when the game asks. Settings › Voice & chat › Mic test shows a bar that moves when you speak.
